Original Series

Entre Mesas

A waitresses’ story for waitresses

Dramatic comedy documentary

An episodic series for teenagers and young adults about a waitress’s first work experience in a high-demand restaurant.

Inspired by real experiences, the series mixes humour, everyday drama and absurd situations to show the reality of working with the public.

Between impossible customers, a rotating staff, rookie mistakes and endless shifts, Sara will have to survive one more summer.

Episode Examples

CHARLIE

A man and his dog are regulars at the restaurant. Every time, he brings a different woman, and the waitresses can’t settle one question: is Charlie the dog or the man?

SIN TIEMPO PARA EL POSTRE

For the first time, our protagonist has to run a dining room on her own. Between calling in tables, taking payments and restocking trolleys, she’ll have to find a way to make it to the dessert station.
